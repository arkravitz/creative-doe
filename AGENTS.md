# Contributor guidance

Keep the engine independent of the UI. Preserve schema snapshots and observation values.
Never invent historical factor values. Distinguish posterior regularization from likelihood
identification. Describe design utilities precisely; do not label heuristics as information gain.
Run pytest and the reproducible music example after changes. Statistical changes need a
simulation or algebraic check and an update to docs/theory.md. No automatic causal claims,
factor retirement, or interaction selection. Keep dependencies small.
